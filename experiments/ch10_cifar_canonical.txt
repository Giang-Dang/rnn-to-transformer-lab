this is the comparison worth printing, and it is not the gap
column above. the absolute gap is free to grow with data while the
CNN still reaches a given accuracy on a fraction of the images -
two different questions, and only the second one is about sample
efficiency. giving the MLP 24 times the parameters moves the
multiplier down and does not remove it.

5. shift stability of the trained models

4      0.3078  0.5446       0.5106
8      0.5981  0.7343       0.7013

read this by column, at one shift. two separate reasons the CNN
is not shift-invariant even though convolution is equivariant:
its head is a Linear over the flattened feature map, so a map
shifted by one cell is a different input vector; and SmallCNN
pads with zeros rather than cyclically, so the exact-zero result
of ch10_equivariance.py does not apply to it at the border - and
a cyclic shift moves content across exactly that border.
