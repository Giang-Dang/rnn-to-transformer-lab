4. the same table read as sample efficiency
   how many images each MLP needs to reach what the CNN reaches

4000            0.5499          over 16,000        over 16,000
16000           0.6399          over 16,000        over 16,000

this is the comparison worth printing, and it is not the gap
column above. the absolute gap is free to grow with data while the
CNN still reaches a given accuracy on a fraction of the images -
two different questions, and only the second one is about sample
efficiency. giving the MLP 24 times the parameters moves the
multiplier down and does not remove it.

the CNN is exactly equivariant at a shift of 8 and still changes
its mind about some images there, because equivariance of the
features is not invariance of the classifier - the head reads
position, and a shifted feature map is a different input to it.
