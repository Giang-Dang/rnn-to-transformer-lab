   CIFAR-10 network this chapter trains

each constraint as a divisor on the parameter count
  locality alone          109.75
  sharing, on top        1024.00
  both together        112384.00

the sharing divisor is the number of spatial positions, 32 x 32.
locality divides by the ratio of receptive fields; sharing divides
by however many places the layer looks. they are not equal partners.

H1 is the layer worth reading twice. the paper writes its own
arithmetic out - 768 biases plus 25 times 12 feature kernels -
and section 3.3 says units do not share their biases, so:
  H1 shared weights                300
  H1 unshared biases               768
  biases as a share of H1       0.7191

and where the network's parameters actually sit:
  H1 + H2, the shared layers     3,660
  H3 + output, fully connected   6,100
  fully connected share         0.6250

3. LeNet-5, rebuilt from LeCun et al. 1998 section II.B
   paper: 340,908 connections, 'only 60,000 trainable' parameters

layer   connections  parameters
C1      122,304      156
S2      5,880        12
C3      151,600      1,516
S4      2,000        32
C5      48,120       48,120
F6      10,164       10,164
output  840          0
TOTAL   340,908      60,000

60,000 is exact rather than rounded. and the connections close
only once the output layer is counted: 10 RBF units of 84 inputs
is 840 connections whose weights the paper fixes by hand and
never trains, so it lands in one total and not the other. leave
it out and the parameters are still 60,000 while the connections
are 340,068, exactly 840 short.

same reading as 1989, and further along:
  C1..S4, the shared layers      1,716
  C5 + F6, fully connected      58,284
  fully connected share         0.9714

the architecture that introduced weight sharing spends 97% of its
parameters in the layers that do not share. sharing did not make
the network small - it made the feature extractor small.
